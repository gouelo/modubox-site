# Accessibility Statement

At Modubox, we want everyone – including people with disabilities – to be able to browse our website, find information and order easily and with dignity. This statement describes the accessibility of our website, in Hebrew and in English.

## Accessibility level

We have worked to adapt this website to the requirements of the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013 (Regulation 35), and Israeli Standard **IS 5568** for web content accessibility, at level **AA**. The standard is based on the W3C's **WCAG 2.0** guidelines. We keep checking and improving the site's accessibility.

## What we've done

- Every part of the site can be used with a keyboard alone, with a clear, highly visible focus indicator.
- A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page.
- A logical heading structure; each page declares its language (Hebrew right-to-left, English left-to-right), and page regions are marked up (header, navigation, main content, footer).
- Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
- Clearly labeled buttons and links, including buttons that show only an icon.
- Color contrast of at least 4.5:1 for regular text and 3:1 for large text.
- Pop-up windows (the mobile menu, the size picker, the photo viewer, the cart and the 3D design panel) open with focus inside them, close with the Esc key and return focus to where you opened them.
- Form fields are labeled, required fields are marked, and error messages are announced to screen readers.
- Content that moves on its own (the reviews and client logos strips) can be paused with a button, and also stops when you hover over it or move focus into it. If you've turned on "reduce motion" on your device, the site shows no animations.
- The site adapts to any screen size, and you can zoom up to 200% without losing content or functionality.
- Every embedded video has a title and only plays when you click it.
- The site was tested with the axe and Lighthouse automated tools, and by hand with a keyboard, in the Chrome browser at phone and desktop sizes.

## Known limitations

Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. These are the ones we know of:

- **The 3D shed designer** runs on an external supplier's website (embedded in a window on our site) and is not under our control. It may not be fully accessible by keyboard or screen reader. Everything it does can be done without it: on each shed's page you can choose the size, color and extras in a regular, accessible form – and you can always contact us and we'll be happy to put together your shed with you.
- **YouTube videos** embedded on the site play in YouTube's own player (a third party). Some videos don't have captions.
- **The map** on the Contact page is an embedded Google map. The address also appears as text, with links to navigate there in Waze and Google Maps.
- WhatsApp messages the site prepares open in the WhatsApp app or website, which are not under our control.
