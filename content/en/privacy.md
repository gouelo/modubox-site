# Privacy Policy

Modubox, a brand of {{legalName}} (company no. {{companyId}}) ("we"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you browse the site, leave your details or order, what we use it for, who we share it with, and your rights. It follows Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, including Amendment 13, and its regulations. The Hebrew version is the binding one.

## What we collect

**What you give us:**

- **Contact form and WhatsApp:** name, phone, town and your message.
- **Orders:** name, phone, email (optional), delivery and installation address, notes, the products you chose and the number of payments.
- **Payment:** you pay on Z-Credit's secure payment page. **Your card details go straight to Z-Credit and never pass through or stay with us.** Z-Credit sends us only the confirmation (amount, approval number, the card's last four digits and type).

You are not legally required to give us these details, but without them we can't get back to you, deliver your order or install your building.

**What is collected automatically:**

- **Cookies and browsing data:** IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit, where you came from, and what you do on the site (for example adding to the cart or tapping WhatsApp). See Cookies below.
- **Your cart** is kept only in your own browser (localStorage) until you order or empty it.
- **Anonymous, cookieless visit statistics:** the site itself counts which pages are viewed, which site you came from (e.g. Google), your device type (phone or computer) and taps on WhatsApp and phone. No IP address, browser details or cookie is stored: only a scrambled identifier that changes every day, so you can't be identified or followed from one day to the next.

## What we use it for

- To get back to you, quote and answer questions.
- To handle your order: confirmation, delivery and installation, service and warranty.
- To meet legal duties such as bookkeeping and invoicing.
- To improve the site (only if you accept measurement cookies).
- To measure our advertising and show relevant ads (only if you accept advertising cookies).
- To send you news and offers, only if you asked for them. You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Who we share it with

We never sell your details. We share them only as needed with Z-Credit (payments), our installers and delivery company (name, phone, address), the service providers that run the site for us (hosting on Vercel, our order system, and WhatsApp when you choose it), Google (and Meta, if enabled) for measurement through Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics and Google Ads, only as your cookie consent allows, and authorities where the law requires it. Some of these providers store data outside Israel (for example in the US and Europe), under the regulations on transferring data abroad.

## Cookies

On your first visit a notice lets you accept all, reject, or choose in Settings: **necessary** cookies (your cart, language and cookie choice; always on), **measurement** (e.g. Google Analytics) and **advertising** (e.g. Google Ads and Meta). Measurement and advertising stay off until you agree (Google Consent Mode). You can change your choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page, or delete cookies in your browser.

## How long we keep it

Inquiries are kept while they are relevant. Order details are kept for the warranty period and as long as the law requires (for example for tax). After that we delete them or keep them without identifying details.

## Security

The site is encrypted (HTTPS), payments are made with a secure payment company, and access to orders is limited to the people who need it. No method is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures under the data security regulations.

## Your rights

You may see the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us not to send you marketing, and withdraw your consent to measurement and advertising cookies. Contact us below. If you feel we haven't handled your request properly, you can contact Israel's Privacy Protection Authority.

## Minors

The site is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please don't give us your details without a parent's consent.
