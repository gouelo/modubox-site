- South of Yeruham – installation by arrangement with the supplier.

1. Access to the installation site must be clear and safe.
2. The delivery location is the address you confirmed in your order.
3. Delivery times are set by the installer only, within a window of up to 4 hours. You'll be notified about half an hour before arrival. Delays are possible if the weather doesn't allow installation.
4. The installer will do their best to accommodate your preferred installation time, as their schedule allows.
5. If you haven't confirmed the arrival time, or we can't reach you on the phone numbers you gave, installation will be postponed to the installer's next available date.
6. If you confirmed a delivery and, for any reason, can't receive it or the shed can't be installed at your home, ₪300 is payable to the installer for delivery and coordination, in addition to the installation payments you committed to.
7. If the installation spot is more than 100 m from where the truck can park, an additional ₪100 per 100 m is payable to the installer.
8. If parts have to be carried up stairs with no elevator, or the product doesn't fit in the elevator, the installer will charge an additional ₪100 per floor. In a building on pillars, the ground floor counts as floor 1. For this calculation, every 17 steps count as one floor, including stairs inside the home.
9. If a crane is needed, its cost is borne by the customer.
10. The shed must stand on a flat, level and compacted surface, and the installation spot must be clear. It's the customer's responsibility to make sure the area is ready before the installer arrives.
11. Cancelling or changing the shed before delivery times are confirmed – no charge.
12. Cancelling or changing the order after delivery times are confirmed – ₪300 is payable to the installer for assembly and delivery services, in addition to the installation payment.
13. A customer who cancels the transaction is credited for the cost of the shed only (delivery costs are not refunded), once it has been returned to the place of purchase, provided it has not been assembled and its parts are in their original packaging. Refunds are made with the supplier's approval only, after the parts have been inspected.
14. Self-delivery, self-assembly or self-dismantling voids the warranty on the product and its parts.
15. Self-collection is available from the Radius company warehouses on Kibbutz Ein Harod Meuhad. Collection times are coordinated with you by Modubox or Radius.
16. When you collect the shed, you check that it is complete, of the right quality and matches your order; with that, Modubox has fulfilled all of its obligations to you.
17. No packing materials or tie-downs are provided, so please come prepared for self-collection.
18. Loading the shed is done by the customer, at the customer's responsibility.
19. A warranty certificate is issued once installation is complete. The warranty terms are on our [Warranty](/warranty) page.
