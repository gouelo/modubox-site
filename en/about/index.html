Our story

Lightweight build, no compromise on looks

At Modubox, we design and manufacture modular structures from light, insulated materials, with a focus on quality, true modularity and carefully considered design.

Our solutions grew out of a real need: smart, durable, good-looking structures that add usable space without a complicated building process.

Let's be honest: structures like these usually aren't much to look at. We put serious thought into how our product looks, and every unit we make is modular – you can play with the size, add windows and doors, and place them wherever you like.

Our engineering team brings more than 35 years of experience in construction, architecture and lightweight building, working with aluminum, polycarbonate and insulated panels – so your unit stands up to the Israeli climate for years.
