Structure & materials

What is a Modubox shed made of?

The frame and profiles are electrostatically coated aluminum, in white or black – they won't rust or rot. The walls and roof are XPV insulated panels, which provide thermal and acoustic insulation and keep out rain, dust and pests. The raised interior floor is made of plastic platforms (an Italian patent), and you can upgrade to WPC flooring.

Skylight windowsAluminum profileXPV insulated panelRaised interior floor
Is it insulated? Is it comfortable in summer and winter?

Yes. The XPV panels, with their insulating core, keep the inside more comfortable in summer and winter and also block noise. For an office, clinic or guest room you can add an inverter AC (together with an electrical panel), and if you want fresh air flowing through there's a ventilation grill.

Can I work, host guests or live in it?

The units are insulated against heat and cold, and customers use them as offices, clinics, studios, home gyms and guest rooms. Water and sewage hookups are done by licensed professionals. Permanent use, as a home or a business, may require approval from your local authority: see the question about building permits.

How much maintenance does it need?

Almost none. Aluminum and insulated panels don't rot or rust, and there's no need to paint or oil anything: just rinse it with water now and then. The WPC flooring also cleans with soap and water.

Sizes, doors & extras

What sizes are there? Can I get a custom size?

The site offers 18 standard sizes, from 1×0.80 m up to 6×3 m, each at a fixed price. Beyond those, custom work is our specialty: half sizes, exact measurements for your yard, and larger structures. Custom sizes are planned with a representative in a personal consultation, where the price is set too.

What does “modular” mean?

The structure is made of parts that connect to each other, so you can order it in a standard size or to exact measurements, put doors and windows wherever you like, and later even enlarge it, shrink it or move it – paying only for the materials and the work.

Where will the door be?

On any side of the shed – your choice. You can swap it for a double door (1.80 m opening) and add another door, a sliding window or a glass wall. You choose all of these in the extras builder on each size's page, or in the 3D configurator.

Can I add electricity and air conditioning?

Yes. The shed's electrical panel is installed at the factory: a residual-current breaker, separate breakers for outlets and lighting, 4 outlets and LED lighting. A licensed electrician connects it to your home's power with a single cable. You can add an inverter AC as an extra (installation not included), and we recommend pairing it with the electrical panel.

03

Price, delivery & installation

What does the price include?

The price on each page is for the structure in the size you chose, including VAT, and it covers the same things on every shed: the aluminum frame, insulated-panel walls and roof, a rigid plastic interior floor, an entry door and skylight windows up top. Sliding windows and glass walls are not in the base price: they are optional extras, like another door, power, AC and WPC parquet flooring, and the price updates in the extras builder. Delivery and installation are priced separately, by size, and the cost appears on each shed's card.

How much do delivery and installation cost?

It depends on the size: the price appears on each shed's card and page, and you choose it in the cart. North of Akko and south of Be'er Sheva there's an extra ₪100, and south of Yeruham it's by arrangement. Under our terms, extra charges may apply if the installation spot is more than 100 m from where the truck can park, if parts must be carried up stairs, or if a crane is needed.

Delivery and installation terms
How soon do you install?

Within 21 business days of receiving your order. The installer arranges an arrival window of up to 4 hours with you and calls about half an hour before arriving, and the assembly itself takes just a few hours.

How do I prepare the site?

The shed stands on a flat, level, firm surface – tiles, concrete, decking and the like – not directly on soil or sand. Preparing the site, and clear, safe access to it, is the customer's responsibility. Don't have a suitable surface? Tell us and we'll advise you on what to prepare.

Do I need a building permit?

It depends on the structure's size, how you use it and where it stands, and the rules vary from one local authority to the next. Before ordering, it's worth checking with your local planning and building committee (va'ada mekomit). We can't give legal advice, but we're happy to give you the unit's dimensions and specifications to check.

Can I pick it up and assemble it myself?

You can collect it yourself from the Radius warehouses on Kibbutz Ein Harod Meuhad, by prior arrangement. Please note: self-transport, self-assembly or dismantling voids the warranty, which is why we recommend installation by our team.

Payment, warranty & cancellation

What warranty do I get?

Modubox sheds come with a 10-year warranty from the date of purchase, covering manufacturing and material defects, as long as the unit was assembled by our installer and stays where it was installed. WPC flooring has a 5-year warranty.

Full warranty terms
How do I pay? Can I pay in installments?

Yes, up to 12 payments. You pay on the site by credit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, on Z-Credit's secure page. You can also order directly on WhatsApp or by phone.

When do I get the invoice?

After you pay, we email you an order confirmation. The tax invoice is handed to you as a paper copy on installation day.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, under the Israeli Consumer Protection Law, with a cancellation fee of ₪100 or 5% of the product's value, whichever is lower. Cancelling or changing an order after the delivery date has been confirmed costs ₪300, paid to the installer.
