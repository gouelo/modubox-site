How to choose a garden shed

Check four things. Size: measure the free space and leave room for the door to open. Insulation and sealing: an insulated shed protects what's inside from damp and extreme temperatures. Durability: an aluminum frame doesn't rust or rot, and never needs painting or oiling. Light: high windows let daylight in, so you don't have to run power to the shed.

Every Modubox garden shed includes all of this in the base price: a white or black aluminum frame, XPV insulated-panel walls and roof, a raised rigid-plastic floor, a wide lockable entry door and skylight windows up top.

Narrow, small and large sheds

For a balcony or the side of the house there are narrow 80 cm deep sheds, 1×0.80 and 2×0.80, which also fit a washer and dryer. Garden tools, bikes and a lawn mower fit sizes from 2×1 to 3×2, and if you also want a work corner there are sizes up to 6×3. Need an exact size for your garden? We also make custom sizes, through a personal consultation with a rep.

Questions and answers

What does a garden shed stand on?

A flat, level, firm surface: tiles, concrete, decking and the like, not bare soil or sand. Preparing the surface is up to you, and if you don't have one, we'll tell you what to prepare.

Will the shed hold up in winter?

Yes. The aluminum frame doesn't rust or rot, and the insulated panels keep out rain, dust and pests and protect what's inside from cold and damp.

Do I need a building permit for a garden shed?

It depends on the size, the use and the location, and the rules vary between local authorities. Check with your local planning and building committee before you order. We're happy to give you the shed's dimensions and specs to check.

How long until my garden shed is installed?

Within 21 business days of receiving your order, and the assembly itself takes a few hours.
