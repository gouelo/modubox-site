Design the shed around your yard

Pick one of 18 fixed sizes, or arrange a custom size with a rep. Choose white or black profiles, and add what you need: a 100×100 cm sliding window, an extra door or a double door with a 1.80 m opening, a glass wall, an electrical panel, a vent grill and a WPC floor. You can see your shed before you order, in the 3D configurator.

For homes and businesses

Customers use backyard sheds for storage, and also as offices, clinics, teen rooms, guest rooms and meeting rooms. Companies, local authorities and institutions have chosen Modubox structures too, from Ben-Gurion University to the Tel Aviv-Yafo municipality.

Installation, warranty and payment

Our team installs the shed within 21 business days of your order, on a flat, level, firm surface such as tiles, concrete or decking. The delivery and installation price is shown with every size. The structure has a 10-year warranty, and you can pay in up to 12 payments.

Questions and answers

Can I choose where the door goes?

Yes, on any side of the shed. You can swap it for a double door (1.80 m opening) and add another door, a sliding window or a glass wall, in the add-ons builder on each size's page or in the 3D configurator.

How much do delivery and installation cost?

It depends on the size: the price is shown with every shed and on its page. North of Akko and south of Be'er Sheva add ₪100, and south of Yeruham by arrangement. Under the terms, extras may apply when the spot is far from parking, involves stairs or needs a crane.

Can a backyard shed be an office?

Yes. It's insulated against heat, cold and noise, and customers use it as an office, clinic or studio. You can add an electrical panel, an inverter air conditioner, a sliding window and a glass wall.
