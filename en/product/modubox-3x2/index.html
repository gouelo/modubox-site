Materials

What is a Modubox shed made of?

Two materials, chosen so the structure stands strong outdoors for years: an aluminum frame and insulated panel walls.

Skylight windowsAluminum profileXPV insulated panelRaised interior floor

Aluminum frame and profiles

The frame and profiles are aluminum, in white or black. Aluminum won't rust or rot, it's light yet rigid, and it keeps its clean look for years.

XPV insulated panels

The walls are XPV insulated panels with an insulating core that keeps the inside more comfortable in summer and winter. A clean, even finish – no painting needed.
