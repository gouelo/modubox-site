How to add them

Pick a size in the shop, and on its page go step by step: doors and windows, power and cooling, interior and floor. The total updates as you go, and at the end you add it to the cart or send it to us on WhatsApp. You can also design your shed in the 3D configurator. Add-ons are sold only with a Modubox shed.

Questions and answers

Can I buy an add-on without a shed?

No. Add-ons are sold only with a Modubox shed, and you choose them on the size's page.

Does the air conditioner include installation?

No. The inverter air conditioner is sold as a shed add-on, without installation, and we recommend pairing it with the electrical panel.

What does the electrical panel include?

A residual-current breaker, separate breakers for sockets and lighting, 4 sockets and LED lighting. It's installed at the factory, and a licensed electrician connects it to your home's power with one cable.
